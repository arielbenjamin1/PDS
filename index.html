Starting something new is a big step. Here's what helps families feel ready.

ASK US SOMETHING ELSE
Does my child need dance experience? +

No. PDS welcomes beginners and provides training through advanced levels. Some classes require assessment, prerequisites, or studio placement.

Which dance styles are offered? +

Offerings listed by PDS include pre-ballet/tap, ballet, jazz, tap, hip hop, contemporary, musical theater, modern, acrobatics, and adult barre. Pointe and several upper-level classes require placement.

How do we find the right class? +

Tell us your dancer's age, experience and interests. Serrah and the studio team can help guide enrollment and confirm current openings.

What should my dancer wear? +

Attire and shoes differ by discipline and level. The current class descriptions provide specific guidelines, and the team can answer questions before you purchase items.

Can we request a trial class? +

Ask PDS directly about trial availability and arrangements by emailing Serrah@dancepds.com. Requests are subject to studio confirmation.

Where is the studio located? +

1664 Anderson Highway, Suite A, Powhatan, VA 23139—serving families from Powhatan and the greater Richmond area. Get directions.
